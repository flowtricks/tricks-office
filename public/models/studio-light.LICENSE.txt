studio-light-stand.glb contains the source tripod, locks, feet, tubes, spigot,
and tilt bracket, extracted and optimized with scripts/prepare-studio-light.mjs.
The dome, ribs, diffusion fabric, amaran-style lamp housing, cable and labels
are custom additions in src/scripts/studio-light.ts based on user photos.
Aputure Light Dome SE and amaran 100d S branding identifies the depicted
real-world equipment; this is not an official manufacturer model.
